# Combined palette evaluation

Run `node --import tsx scripts/palette-audit.ts` to reproduce the checked-in reference dataset, simulation sheet, and numeric report in `audits/`. These artifacts are generated from the same exported palette and `metricColor` function used by the map and legend, not a separately maintained swatch list. The reference date is 2026-10-05 12:00 UTC; reading counts are 1, 2, 5, 10, 25, 50 and ages are 400, 120, 45, 14, 3, 0 days. All 36 combinations are evaluated alongside unrecorded gray.

The sheet includes the minimum and default whole-Bible stride of 4px (3px fills), focused strides of 8px and 14px, enlarged swatches, normal sRGB, severe protan/deutan simulations, an approximate tritan simulation, grayscale, and a washed-out display proxy (30% blend toward the map background). Matrices are applied to linear sRGB and encoded/clipped for display. Source: [Machado, Oliveira and Fernandes (2009)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19834201/) and [Colour's published precomputed matrices](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/colour-science/colour/develop/colour/blindness/datasets/machado2010.py). [Colour's method documentation](https://colour.readthedocs.io/en/master/_modules/colour/blindness/machado2009.html) explains that tritan simulation is an approximation rather than an accurate tritanopia model.

Frequency ordering is checked using linear-sRGB relative luminance for each recency category in every simulated condition. Adjacent recency differences and the distance from lightly recorded cells to gray use CIELAB Delta E 1976. A distance below 10 is flagged for visual review; this is a conservative screening heuristic, not a perceptual guarantee or accessibility threshold. The report lists every difficult pair rather than discarding them.

The refined palette reduces lightest-cell lightness from 80% to 70%, increases saturation, expands the green end of the hue range, and maintains monotonic frequency darkness. This improves separation from gray and spreads neighboring normal-vision hues. Legend labels now state the exact implemented boundaries (365+, 90–364, 30–89, 7–29, 1–6 days, today / under 24 hours). Book highlighting traces the external boundary in the cell gaps instead of outlining every fill; the inspected cell uses black/white rings. Base metric fills remain visible.

## Interpretation and limitations

At 4px, color indicates broad patterns rather than reliable six-bucket classification. Normal hues are more useful at focused scales, but adjacent older brown/yellow categories and adjacent green categories can still be hard to distinguish at low lightness. Protan/deutan simulation compresses the brown-to-green encoding; grayscale cannot identify recency at equal frequency. The numeric report records the specific problematic pairs across frequency levels. Unrecorded gray is separate from recorded cells in these reference simulations, but washed-out or poorly calibrated displays reduce distinctions.

The simulation sheet must be inspected at native scale; enlarging a screenshot does not recreate a larger map cell. These tests model sRGB, not physical brightness, ambient glare, individual vision, or every display. They do not establish that color alone works for every viewer. Exact references, counts, and last-read dates remain available through keyboard, touch details, and the textual inspector. Standalone Recency encodes age through lightness; Frequency encodes count through lightness. The legend states these alternatives directly.

The native-scale reference sheet was visually inspected in Chromium on macOS (1080×1260 CSS pixels). All frequency columns darken visibly in the normal sheet. At 4px the middle recency categories cluster; at 8px/14px normal hues separate more clearly. Protan/deutan collapse several green/yellow neighbors; grayscale is unsuitable for recency classification. The lightest recorded grayscale cell is only Delta E 6.33 from unrecorded gray, so that distinction remains fragile despite the refinement. Severe tritan simulation and washed-out normal simulation produce additional flagged pairs. These observed limitations are retained in the report and disclosed in the legend.
