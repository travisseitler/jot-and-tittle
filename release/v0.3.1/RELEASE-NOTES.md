Jot & Tittle v0.3.1 makes app guidance easier to find and understand, from your first reading to backups and journal recovery.

[Open the app](https://travisseitler.github.io/jot-and-tittle/app/) · [Read the guide](https://travisseitler.github.io/jot-and-tittle/)

### Highlights

- Clearer first-use instructions and explanations of the Bible map, with less repeated slogan text in working screens.
- Sample readings and Verse list labels that make their purpose easier to recognize.
- Help explaining reading counts, overlapping passages, patterns, and copies between journals.
- Clearer empty states for filters and archived journals, with recovery guidance.
- More direct guidance for backups, imports, browser storage, and display settings.

Existing journals and readings continue to use database schema 3 and backup format 3. Readings stay in your browser; export all journals to back up or transfer your history.

### Downloads

- **jot-and-tittle-build.zip**: Ready-to-host production files, a license, deployment instructions, and matching embedded source. Extract and serve over HTTPS or localhost.
- **jot-and-tittle-source.zip**: Application source, documentation, scripts, and tests. Development requires Node.js 22 or later; production builds also require Python 3.
- GitHub's automatic source archives require a build before static hosting.

The app uses the 66-book Protestant canon and KJV verse numbering. It includes no Bible text or cloud sync.
